# Ken Roy Cardanio — Core Values

Constitution for this site and for paid work offered from it.  
If a public claim or a client promise violates these, it does not ship.

**Tagline:** *One useful task. Proof you can see. Pay for the finished job.*

**Portfolio direction:** The public page is a concise employer profile. Headline: Turn efficiency into something real. Supporting line: Business understanding. Technology-driven solutions. It presents Ken’s capabilities, experience, and approach. It is not a project catalog, a product site, or a freelance menu. Past work may inform a capability description. Do not present an unfinished application as a completed product.

Income goal: work that can sustain Ken. Two doors, one person.

1. **Services first.** Schools and small shops pay for a finished task he can already do.
2. **A staff role stays open** when the job is the same kind of work.
3. **Life planning stays in its own lane.** It is an AIA Philippines agent practice, not a software feature and not the corporate AIA page.

---

## 1. Values

### One useful outcome
- Each offer does one job a client can use this month.
- The page sells the result, not a stack of tools or a personality.

### Plain speech
- Say what it does, what it needs from the client, and how to start.
- No soft fog, no invented job title, no fake years of employment.

### Their desk
- Start from the sheet, form, Photoshop file, or daily log they already use.
- Do not ask them to abandon that system for a new one they do not need.

### Separate lanes
- Office tools, a staff role, and life planning do not share claims.
- Life planning: agent practice since 2019 (OL & VUL). No rates, benefits, returns, or guarantees on this site. Not corporate AIA.

### No invented proof
- Only show work that exists. Threadkeeper may be described only for behavior that has been built and run. Vault may appear as earlier work, not as the lead product. Unifield stays off the site.
- Prices are agreed per job. They are not published until Ken sets them.

### Finish the scoped job
- Do the agreed task before adding a second system.
- No “full company platform” promise on a first conversation.

### Ken owns the claim
- Drafts can be prepared here. Public promises about money, insurance, or results wait for Ken.

## 3. How the page earns the visit

Beauty that does not communicate the offer is unfinished. Build the page as one experience.

- The first screen shows what is for sale and who it is for.
- Each section has one job: offer, skill, record, or contact.
- Trust comes from work that exists. A missing proof is a gap to name, not a claim to invent.
- Contact is an obvious next step, with little to fill in.
- The layout is distinctive and restrained: type, space, and contrast do the work. No template look, no effect without a purpose.
- Phone first, then desktop. Readable type, enough contrast, keyboard access, fast load, and reduced motion when the visitor asks for it.
